 it's above hi you get hi. Otherwise you get n back.                                                                   │
                                                                                                                       │
 It's three comparisons in src/clamp.js. The lower check runs first, then the upper, then it falls through to n. A     │
 value exactly on a bound comes back unchanged. lo === hi always returns that one value.                               │

 It does no validation, so a few inputs behave in ways you might not expect:                                           │
 - NaN: it fails both comparisons, so you get NaN back.                                                                │
